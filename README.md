# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis (Python)

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn  |  **Type:** Data cleaning + EDA + business insights

## Business Problem
A retail company (5 categories, 4 regions) wants to know *what drives revenue and where profit is leaking*, so management can decide on discount policy, regional focus and festive-season planning.

## Dataset
~7,000 order records (Jan 2023 - Dec 2024): order date, customer, region, segment, category, price, quantity, discount, sales, profit.
> Data is **synthetic** (generated by `generate_data.py`, seed=42) with realistic seasonality, discounts and deliberately injected dirt (duplicates and missing values). To use real data, swap in the Kaggle "Superstore Sales" dataset and rename the columns.

## Approach
1. **Cleaning:** removed 25 duplicate rows, imputed 100 missing region/segment values as `Unknown`, parsed dates, created year/month/profit-margin features.
2. **KPIs:** total sales, profit, margin %, AOV, order count.
3. **Analysis:** category, region, segment, monthly trend, discount-vs-margin, customer concentration, category x region profit heatmap.

## Key Insights (from `insights.txt`)
- Electronics = **57% of revenue**, but Clothing has the best margin (**25%**) while Furniture is weakest (**3%**).
- **Discounts destroy profit:** average margin drops from **17.4% (no discount) to about -0.4% at 30% discount**; **12.5% of orders are loss-making**.
- **Oct-Dec (festive) quarter = ~30% of total sales** -> plan inventory and staffing ahead.
- **Top 10% customers = ~28% of revenue**.
- North is the top region; East is the weakest.

## Recommendations
Cap discounts at 10-20% on Furniture/Electronics, push high-margin Clothing in festive campaigns, and run loyalty offers for top customers.

```
pip install -r requirements.txt

```
